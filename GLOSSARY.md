# Daily Signals

Daily Signals records personal lifestyle events and symptom observations for later analysis.

## Language

**Event**:
A timestamped record of a lifestyle occurrence or symptom observation.

**Event ID**:
The stable identity of an event across edits, backups, and imports.

**Deleted event**:
An event removed from normal history whose deletion remains recorded so that older backups do not restore it accidentally.

**Backup**:
A portable copy of recorded events, including deleted events, that can be imported for restoration or analysis.
